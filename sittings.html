An Orchard Learning the Piano · Brooklyn · 40.68°N · DREAM

01 · DREAM

Paint My Dream

You tell me one real dream, or a half-remembered image that keeps coming back, in a 75-minute video sitting. I paint it as one finished original in my own surreal language, in the medium I choose. It’s not a storyboard sketch, and it’s not a copy of your face.

You receive: the original painting, a short written legend of its symbols, and photos of the process.

Artist’s choice

Support

From USD 4,500 (24×30) · from USD 8,500 (36×48). Shipping extra.

Not this: vacation as Dalí, other artists’ characters, digital file as the main deliverable.

Time: sitting + 4–8 weeks in studio.

Fox and Heron, Seated · Oaxaca · 17.07°N · SIGHT

02 · SIGHT

Presence Portrait

A portrait of you, or of two people, that’s allowed to stop looking like you. We sit first. Then I paint your presence: rooms, animals, weather and objects standing in for you, the way a person comes back to you at the edge of sleep. Your face can appear, but it doesn’t have to.

You receive: the original painting and a one-page legend. The legend is my reading of the sitting, not a psychic certificate.

Artist’s choice

oil · acrylic · gouache · watercolor

Support

canvas · watercolor on large paper

From USD 5,000 (24×30) · from USD 9,000 (36×48).

Not this: corporate headshot, memorial exact likeness as fact, channeled-angel template.

Time: sitting + 4–8 weeks.

Supper at the Altitude of Dusk · Santa Fe · 35.69°N · NIGHT

03 · NIGHT

Live Event Painting

I come to a wedding, a dinner or a salon for one evening and paint the room as it feels. Then I finish the painting in my studio and ship it to you or hand it over.

You receive: one original event painting (typical 24×36 or 30×40).

Artist’s choice

oil · acrylic · gouache · watercolor

Support

canvas · watercolor on large paper

From USD 4,000 + airfare, hotel, per diem. Date must sit in a published travel season unless you pay a voyage premium.

Not this: guest caricatures all night, photo-real first kiss on demand, same-week turnaround.

Time: 1 evening on site + 2–4 weeks studio finish.

The Moon-Door · Mérida · 20.97°N · ROOM

04 · ROOM

Dream Mural

We sit, and then I paint a mural by hand on a real wall. Once you approve the composition, the design is fixed. Anything I fix at the end is measured against that composition, not “I changed my mind about blue.”

You receive: finished wall + care notes + photo documentation.

Artist’s choice

oil · acrylic · gouache · watercolor

Support

canvas · watercolor on large paper

From USD 10,000 + travel + site costs. Large or trompe-l’œil walls quote higher.

Not this: printed wallpaper, flying for a canvas that could have been shipped.

Time: design 2–3 weeks · on site 1–3 weeks.
